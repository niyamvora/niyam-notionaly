# Characters

Notion-style characters are **ordinary people**, not a mascot. There is no fixed IP to
reuse. Consistency comes from construction rules, so any character you draw belongs to the
same world.

## Proportions

- Roughly 6 to 7 heads tall. Adult, natural, slightly elongated legs.
- Head is a soft oval, a little wider at the cranium, tapering to a small rounded chin.
- Shoulders narrow. Limbs are simple tubes with no muscle detail.
- Hands are simplified — a mitten mass with one or two finger separations. Never five
  articulated fingers.
- Feet are small, in solid black shoes, often just a wedge shape.

## Face — keep it nearly empty

- **Eyes:** two short downward arcs (closed, content) or two small filled dots. Arcs are
  the default and the more characteristic choice.
- **Nose:** one tiny hook or a short curve. Often omitted in profile.
- **Mouth:** a small curve. Sometimes a tiny open oval when speaking.
- **Brows:** only when an expression needs them.
- No blush, no freckles, no eyelashes, no pupils with highlights, no teeth.

The face carries almost no information. Posture and gesture carry the emotion.

## Hair — this is where the character lives

Hair is the main identifying feature and the main **solid black mass**. Make it generous
and specific: tight curls drawn as stacked spirals, a voluminous bob, a topknot, a bun
with loose strands, a fade, a wrap or hijab, a long sweep past the shoulder.

Draw hair as a confident filled silhouette with a few white negative-space curls cut into
it. Never draw individual strands.

## Skin

Skin tone is expressed as **ink opacity**, never colour:

- Left white (unfilled)
- `#231F20` at ~20% for a mid tone

That is the entire range. Two figures in one drawing with different values reads as
diversity without introducing colour.

## Clothing

The value rhythm that makes the style work:

- **Top:** white, outlined. Loose shirt, sweater, jacket, blouse.
- **Bottom:** solid black. Trousers, wide-leg pants, skirt, dress.
- **Shoes:** solid black.

Occasionally invert it (black top, white bottom) for the second figure in a pair so the two
read apart. Add one small detail only — a button line, a cuff, a collar, a pocket.

Clothes are loose and drape. No tight fits, no logos, no patterns beyond simple stripes.

## Posture

This is where all the expression is. Lean into the action: mid-stride with trailing leg,
weight shifted onto one hip, shoulders hunched over a laptop, arm extended reaching, torso
twisted to look back. Avoid symmetrical standing poses — they look dead in this style.

## Reusing a character across images

To keep one person consistent across a set, lock and restate these five every time:

1. Hair silhouette (the most recognisable)
2. Skin value (white or 20%)
3. Top colour (white or black) and its one detail
4. Bottom colour
5. One accessory, if any — glasses, earring, bag

Write them into every prompt verbatim. Do not rely on the model remembering.
