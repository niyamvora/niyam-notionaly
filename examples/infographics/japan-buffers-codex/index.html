Planning a trip to Japan

Japan doesn't reward itineraries.
It rewards buffers.

Trips don't fall apart at the attractions. They fall apart in the seams between them.

Last train, Tokyo

12:00–12:40am

A typical final window, not a suggestion. Miss it and the article's example is a ¥9,000 taxi.

Luggage forwarding

¥2,000–2,500

Takkyūbin sends your case ahead. A transfer day becomes a normal day.

Kyoto day trips

under 90min

Nara, Osaka, Himeji, Uji and Kobe. One base, six ways.

Split your days before you fill them

Anchor days
~50%
Drift days
~50%

Anchor days have one fixed booking. Drift days have nothing booked, so nothing can be late. A good two-week trip is roughly half and half.

Where a day actually breaks

Late start
Elastic hours
Last train

Protect the morning and the last train. Let everything between them bend.
