# Shot list — Japan Doesn't Reward Itineraries. It Rewards Buffers.

Source: [`examples/japan-buffers.md`](../../japan-buffers.md). Six shots for a ~1,400-word
article. One is drawn; the rest are planned.

> with a single placket line, solid black trousers, small solid black wedge shoes, one grey
> backpack (ink at 20%).

### 01 · the bag goes ahead ✅
- **Placement:** after "The bag takes the boring route. You take the interesting one."
- **Idea:** you walk the day unburdened because the suitcase went on without you
- **Structure:** flow
- **Figure:** mid-stride, walking right, away from the case, carrying only a backpack
- **Props:** upright suitcase (left behind), small backpack
- **Caption:** The suitcase takes the boring route
- **Files:** `01-bag-goes-ahead.svg` (source), `01-bag-goes-ahead.png` (1600x900)
- **Measured:** ink 11.7% of subject bbox · edge/ink 0.25 · solid mass 75% · 0 chromatic px

### 02 · the morning that already went
- **Placement:** after "You are now eighty minutes behind, and it's the first thing you did today."
- **Idea:** the day is lost before it starts
- **Structure:** states — three small vignettes of the same person
- **Figure:** holding a coffee, one shoe on, looking at nothing
- **Props:** a cup, a single shoe
- **Caption:** Eighty minutes behind, before anything began

### 03 · the countdown nobody scheduled
- **Placement:** after "Every evening in Japan quietly runs a countdown clock that your itinerary doesn't show."
- **Idea:** the last train is a wall, not a preference
- **Structure:** concept object — the one permitted exaggeration
- **Figure:** dwarfed by a clock, reaching past it rather than at it
- **Props:** one oversized clock face
- **Caption:** The last train is not a suggestion

### 04 · seams, not stops
- **Placement:** after "It's 'spend your planning on the seams instead of the stops.'"
- **Idea:** the same day, packed tight or given room
- **Structure:** before and after — carried by posture, no divider
- **Figure:** hunched over a crumpled sheet on the left; upright with it flat on the right
- **Props:** one sheet of paper
- **Caption:** Same day, different amount of room

### 05 · anchor days and drift days
- **Placement:** after "...it's a logistics job you assigned yourself on vacation."
- **Idea:** half your days should carry nothing fixed
- **Structure:** layers
- **Figure:** setting a crate down rather than adding one to the stack
- **Props:** three crates, uneven
- **Caption:** Half the days should hold nothing

### 06 · the flip on day four
- **Placement:** after "...start being a person who lives here temporarily and has errands to run."
- **Idea:** you stop executing a list and start running errands
- **Structure:** path
- **Figure:** mid-stride, hands loose, a tote instead of a map
- **Props:** a tote bag
- **Caption:** Day four, and you have errands
