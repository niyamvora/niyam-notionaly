# Places — Codex prompts

Ten landmarks along the Tokyo → Kyoto → Osaka route, each drawn in the Notionaly style with
the same traveller in every image. Paste one block into Codex per image. Reference photos
and their licences live in [`reference-photos/`](reference-photos/CREDITS.md).

Every prompt follows one rule: **the landmark becomes the oversized object**. That is the only
exaggeration the style permits, and it replaces the background scenery the style forbids.
Keep one to three elements that make the place recognisable and drop everything else.
Colour, crowds and signage all go.

| # | Place | City | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Shibuya Crossing | Tokyo | mid-stride across the stripes |
| 02 | Kaminarimon | Tokyo | looking straight up at the lantern |
| 03 | Tokyo Tower | Tokyo | aiming a camera up |
| 04 | Meiji Jingu torii | Tokyo | bowing before the gate |
| 05 | Fushimi Inari | Kyoto | climbing through the gate tunnel |
| 06 | Arashiyama bamboo | Kyoto | hand trailing across a stalk |
| 07 | Ryōan-ji | Kyoto | sitting on the veranda edge |
| 08 | Fushimi canal boat | Kyoto | seated in the boat, drifting |
| 09 | Byōdō-in | Uji | holding a coin up against the temple |
| 10 | Dotonbori | Osaka | leaning on the railing, eating takoyaki |

Every action is different, so the set never repeats itself. 04 and 05 both have torii, but
one is a single gate and a bow and the other is a tunnel and a climb.

PLACE: Shibuya Scramble Crossing, Tokyo

SUBJECT: the traveller mid-stride across the crossing, head lifted, the moment after the lights change.

KEEP FROM THE PHOTO: five or six broad zebra stripes, laid at the angle the photo shows them.

DROP: the crowd, every building, billboard, screen, bus, car and crane.

CHARACTER — keep identical across the whole places set: shoulder-length black bob with a side sweep past the jaw, skin left white, loose white shirt with a single placket line, solid black trousers, small solid black wedge shoes, a small backpack in ink at 20%.

RULES: #231F20 ink only. No colour anywhere, even though the photo is full of it. Tapered brush strokes, solid black hair and trousers, ink about 10% of the canvas, subject about 60% of the frame, nothing touching the edge. No scenery beyond the landmark elements named above. No text, letters, numbers or signage anywhere. 1:1, 1024x1024, solid white background, PNG.
